Questions, answered

When do I get access after paying?

Access is activated manually. Once your payment is verified, your email is added and you can sign in to the web app with it. Use the same email you entered at checkout.

Is it a subscription?

No. The price is a single once-off payment of R1,000 for lifetime access to the workspace.

Does SignalsPro tell me what to trade?

No. It is an analysis workspace that helps you study zones, plan levels and keep macro context in view. It is not financial advice and does not guarantee results.

What devices does it work on?

Android phones use the SignalsPro Android app. iPhone, iPad and computers use the web app in a modern browser, with nothing to install.

How do I install the Android app?

Download the APK from this page and open it. Because it does not come from the Play Store, Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager for this one time, and it may show a warning. Allow it, install, then sign in with the email you used at checkout.
