Trade plan
Load a chart to see entries and targets.
Limit entries sit a little in front of the zone so near-misses still fill. When a reaction candle closes at the zone the plan switches to ENTER NOW at the market price instead of waiting for a pullback. Entries more than 2 ATR away are shown as watch-only. Stop: a quarter ATR beyond the far edge of the zone. Targets: the next opposite zones (or 1.5R and 3R). Not financial advice.

Market Scanner
Each symbol uses 1 data credit and takes about 8 seconds, to stay inside the free 8-per-minute limit.
Each market is classed as trending or ranging (efficiency ratio plus the EMA20/EMA50 gap). Trending markets get pullback trades with the trend, and ranging markets get bounce trades between the zones. Ranking = regime fit, movement versus normal, zone strength, trade-plan quality, trading session, optional higher-timeframe agreement, candle confirmation (a reaction candle closed at the zone) and currency strength. Not backtested and not financial advice.
